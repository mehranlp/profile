The approach

Built around process and risk.

The strategy combines systematic signals with explicit risk appetite decisions and ongoing oversight. The objective is a repeatable process—not a discretionary trade-by-trade narrative.

01 / Research

Independent development

Designed and tested on historical spot FX data, with a documented backtest separate from subsequent live trading results.

02 / Execution

Automated trading

Fully automated execution with a daily trading frequency and positions may be held for several days, depending on market regime.

03 / Oversight

Dynamic risk management

Volatility-aware position sizing and discretionary adjustments to risk appetite, concentration, and exposure around important events.
